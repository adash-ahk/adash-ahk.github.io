# adash.ahk

[adash.ahk](https://github.com/adash-ahk/adash.ahk) is a free and open source utility library for AutoHotkey, with zero dependencies. It brings a modern, immutable toolkit for arrays, objects, strings, and more to everyday scripts.

## Why donate?

adash.ahk is free, and it is developed and maintained by independent volunteers. There is no company or ad revenue behind it.

All of our costs are met by donations we receive from our users. If you enjoy using adash.ahk, please donate to support us.

## What do donations pay for?

- adding new features
- writing new tests
- maximising performance
- expanding documentation
